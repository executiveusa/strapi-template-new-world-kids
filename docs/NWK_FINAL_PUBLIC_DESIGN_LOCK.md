# New World Kids — Final Public Design Lock

Status: LOCKED FOR COPY / OPEN FOR VERIFIED MEDIA

## Public design rule

The site is editorial, documentary, and media-led. It should feel like a living field journal rather than a SaaS product, sports brand, or nonprofit template.

The final visual pass favors asymmetric editorial composition, staggered media rhythm, restrained typography, offset navigation, and deliberate empty space. It should feel authored rather than templated.

## Color system

- Paper: `#F2EEE5`
- Bright paper: `#FBF9F4`
- Ink: `#191714`
- Soft ink: `#302D29`
- Botanical neutral: `#70756B`
- Muted text: `#706B63`
- Warm dark field/footer: `#1E1A17`

Strong color should come primarily from photography and film. The botanical neutral is reserved for small labels, focus states, and quiet wayfinding—not large brand blocks. Large dark fields use warm ink rather than green so the paper, typography, and future media do not compete.

## Typography

- Geist is the public type system.
- Headlines use strong weight, tight tracking, and compact line-height.
- Body copy stays restrained, readable, and narrow enough for scanning.
- Eyebrows are small, letter-spaced, and quiet.
- No decorative display font is added.

## Spacing and composition

- Large sections use generous vertical rhythm: roughly 96–144px on desktop and 80–96px on mobile/tablet depending on density.
- Thin rules divide ideas instead of cards.
- Content is aligned to a consistent max-width editorial grid.
- White space is intentional and should not be filled merely to make a section feel busy.
- Story/media layouts may stagger vertically and horizontally to create an editorial sequence rather than a uniform gallery grid.
- Reserved media areas must look intentionally staged, with explicit `Reserved for` labeling and a clear description of the future footage.

## Story pairings

The archive treats the two major chapters as explicit editorial pairings:

- `NEW WORLD KIDS X INDIGO AZUL PROJECT`
- `NEW WORLD KIDS X FIRST 12`

These pairings are visual/editorial labels only. They do not merge the provenance of the two communities or imply that Seattle outcomes already exist.

## Footer

The footer is a warm dark editorial field, not a utility strip.

- Primary navigation is set as large offset links that step diagonally across the composition.
- The main statement is also staggered to create a designed closing cadence.
- Story/contact links remain text-forward.
- Fiscal sponsorship remains visible but deliberately quiet.
- No social icon buttons, card containers, or dashboard-style columns.

## Interaction

- Links use editorial rules and directional arrows.
- No pill-heavy controls.
- No card grids on the public story journey unless a functional exception is required.
- Social destinations are presented as text-forward editorial links, not generic social icon buttons.
- Motion should be subtle and purposeful.
