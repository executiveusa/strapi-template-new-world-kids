# New World Kids safeguarding policy (DRAFT for counsel and HSI review)

Status: draft. Not approved. Do not publish until a lawyer familiar with Washington law and HSI have
reviewed it. Items in [brackets] are decisions we have not made.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy protects young people ages 16 to 19 who take part in the First 12 pilot (referral only, King, Snohomish, Pierce, and Skagit counties), and the staff, mentors, and
project hosts who work with them. It applies to everyone who has contact with participants.

## 2. Who may work with participants

- Mentors and project hosts are approved before any contact with a participant.
- Screening: Washington State Patrol background checks under RCW 43.43.830 to 43.43.838 for every mentor, host contact, and staff member who may have access to a participant, plus [FBI fingerprint check?], [provider], and [renewal period]. Counsel to confirm which checks are required and which are policy.
- Two adults rule: [no one is alone with a participant out of sight of others, or another rule].
- References: [number and type].

## 3. Projects

Each project is reviewed for fit, safety, supervision, timing, and pathway before it is offered.
Every project has a named supervisor who is present during the work. Work must be lawful for the
participant's age. For 16 and 17 year olds that means the hours, authorization, permit, and prohibited-duty rules
in `docs/NWK_YOUTH_EMPLOYMENT_COMPLIANCE.md`.

## 4. Conduct

Mentors and hosts must not: be alone with a participant privately online or off-site, give gifts or
money outside the program, share a participant's information, or take photos or video without the
written consent in section 7. [Add alcohol, drugs, and social-media rules.]

## 5. Reporting concerns

- Anyone who suspects abuse or neglect must tell [named safeguarding lead] right away.
- Washington law (RCW 26.44.030) requires listed professionals to report suspected child abuse or neglect, and
  separately requires any person in an official supervisory role at a nonprofit to report suspected abuse by a
  person they supervise who coaches, trains, educates, counsels, or has unsupervised access to a child, to law
  enforcement. For us, that means program staff and mentors. Participants who are 16 or 17 are children for this
  purpose. Print the current DCYF reporting number from dcyf.wa.gov/safety/mandated-reporter, confirmed by counsel.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is not allowed.
- The lead tells HSI [within what time] of any serious incident.

## 6. Handling a report

Record facts only; protect the participant; do not investigate alone; keep records confidential;
cooperate with authorities. [Who decides to remove a mentor or host, and how.]

## 7. Photos, video, and information

No participant name, school, schedule, or identifiable image is published without written consent from
the participant and, if under 18, a parent or guardian. Consent can be withdrawn. Consent forms are
kept by [owner]. Proyecto Indigo Azul footage is never presented as Seattle participant activity.
