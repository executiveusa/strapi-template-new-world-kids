# Indigo Azul year archive

This slice keeps the Proyecto Indigo Azul gallery visibly organized by capture year from 2021 through 2026.

## Source rule

Only confirmed, capture-dated field images from `timelineEntries` are placed into a year. A timeline chapter's multi-year label is not used to infer an image capture year.

## Archive years

- 2021
- 2022
- 2023
- 2024
- 2025
- 2026

Years without a confirmed capture-dated image remain visible and ready for verified media.
