## Already done in the design pass (not proposals)

Headings now follow one scale (A12). Eyebrow labels are 12px or larger (A13). One filled primary action per
view (competing-actions flag). Press feedback and a 14px rise on entry, with reduced-motion off switch.

## Order I would do them in

A1, A2, A3 first (one afternoon). Then E1 and E2. Then E5, which is the biggest gap for the people the program serves.
E3 and E6 only after you decide on the locked homepage copy.
