# 02_grant-research — find and qualify grant opportunities

One job: produce a short list of funders that genuinely fit a current New World Kids program need.

## Inputs
- Reference: `../_shared/mission.md`
- Reference: `../_shared/legal-and-fiscal-sponsor.md`
- Reference: `../_shared/agent-automation-policy.md`
- Working: current program priorities, geography, eligible applicant structure, budget range, and deadlines.

## Process
1. Search public/authorized sources only; record source URL and date.
2. For GrantStation, do not automate the member site. Accept human-entered/exported opportunity details and normalize them here.
3. Check eligibility before scoring: geography, applicant type/fiscal sponsorship, program area, deadline, amount, and restrictions.
4. Score fit on mission match, evidence readiness, administrative burden, award size, timing, and relationship opportunity.
5. Reject weak fits explicitly instead of drafting everything.

## Outputs
- `output/opportunities.md`
- `output/shortlist.md`

Each shortlisted record must include: funder, opportunity, source, deadline, amount/range, eligibility, restrictions, program match, evidence needed, contact/funder-question opportunity, and confidence.

## Human check
Owner chooses which opportunities move to drafting. A shortlist is research, not permission to apply.
