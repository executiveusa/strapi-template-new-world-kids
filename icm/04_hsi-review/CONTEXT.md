# 04_hsi-review — obtain fiscal-sponsor review before submission

One job: produce a clean review packet for Humanitarian Social Innovations and record the result.

## Inputs

- Working: `../03_grant-drafting/output/grant-draft.md`
- Working: `../03_grant-drafting/output/claim-ledger.md`
- Working: `../03_grant-drafting/output/attachments-checklist.md`
- Reference: `../_shared/legal-and-fiscal-sponsor.md`

## Process

1. Assemble the exact application draft, budget, deadline, funder link, requested amount, and attachment list.
2. Identify any representations about fiscal sponsorship, tax status, legal name, banking, ownership of funds, or reporting obligations.
3. Send/prepare the packet through HSI's current review channel; do not submit the grant to the funder first.
4. Record HSI feedback verbatim enough to act on it, revise the draft, and resubmit to HSI if changes are requested.
5. Only mark the packet approved when HSI approval is actually received.

## Outputs

- `output/hsi-review-packet.md`
- `output/hsi-review-status.md`

`hsi-review-status.md` must contain: opportunity, version reviewed, reviewer, date, status (`pending|approved|changes-requested`), required changes, and final submission deadline.

## Human check

Owner verifies that `status: approved` reflects real HSI approval. No agent may bypass this gate because of grant size or deadline pressure.
