# New World Kids — mission context

## Core mission

New World Kids teaches practical life skills through real projects organized around four pillars: **food, water, energy, and shelter**.

## Current program frame

- **Culture Shock — Seattle:** practical life-skills work connected to mentorship, art, sports, and urban agriculture.
- **Proyecto Indigo Azul — Puerto Vallarta / rural Mexico:** field-based food-forest demonstration and practical-systems learning.

## Public-story rule

The site should move in this order: **clarity → proof → programs → dated field evidence → trust/public records → support → earned-revenue services**.

## Funding model

New World Kids uses charitable fundraising for mission work and keeps paid services structurally separate from charitable giving. The fiscal sponsor relationship is with Humanitarian Social Innovations (HSI).

## Evidence discipline

Do not invent impact numbers, participant counts, acreage, years, savings, tax language, grant outcomes, or testimonials. A fact is publishable only when its source is known and the repository marks it as usable for that purpose.
