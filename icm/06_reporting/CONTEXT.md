# 06_reporting — close the loop with evidence

One job: turn fundraising and grant activity into a verified operating report and next decision.

## Inputs
- Working: FundRazr transaction/report exports or authorized integration output
- Working: channel/UTM analytics
- Working: grant statuses and HSI review records
- Working: campaign costs and paid-media spend

## Process
1. Reconcile gross contributions, fees, net amount, donor count, recurring gifts, refunds/failed payments, and date range.
2. Break traffic/conversions down by campaign and UTM channel when the data supports it.
3. Separate fundraising results from grant pipeline results; submitted is not awarded and pledged is not received.
4. Record what messages/assets/channels worked, what failed, and what is still unknown.
5. Feed only verified lessons back into the next campaign or grant-research run.

## Outputs
- `output/fundraising-report.md`
- `output/grant-pipeline-report.md`
- `output/lessons.md`

## Human check
Owner verifies the reported totals against source exports/accounts before numbers become public claims or planning assumptions.
