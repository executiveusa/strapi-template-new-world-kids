# 05_campaign-traffic — drive qualified traffic to approved campaigns

One job: turn an approved FundRazr campaign into a measurable distribution plan without inventing urgency or impact.

## Inputs
- Working: `../01_fundraising/output/campaign-copy.md`
- Working: approved FundRazr URL
- Reference: `../_shared/mission.md`
- Reference: `../_shared/agent-automation-policy.md`
- Working: approved images/video, donor list permissions, social accounts, and channel history.

## Process
1. Build channel-specific messages for email, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, partner outreach, and the NW Kids site.
2. Use one canonical campaign URL with UTM tags per channel/campaign wave.
3. Schedule a launch, proof/update, reminder, and close/continuation sequence; do not spam or manufacture scarcity.
4. Reuse field evidence only when rights and factual context are known.
5. Route replies requiring judgment, promises, refunds, tax advice, or sponsor interpretation to a human.

## Outputs
- `output/channel-plan.md`
- `output/content-queue.md`
- `output/utm-map.md`

## Human check
Owner approves the outbound sequence, audiences, and any paid spend before agents publish or send at scale.
